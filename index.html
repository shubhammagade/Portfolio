The person behind the iFlows

Curious about systems. Careful with data.

My work sits between SAP and everything it has to talk to: banks that expect a payment file on time, tax portals that reject a malformed invoice, and legacy PI/PO interfaces that need a new home.

At NoblQ I design and build CPI iFlows using REST, SOAP, OData, XML and JSON, and I extend SAP Business ByDesign with the SDK, ABSL and custom business objects. I care about the unglamorous parts: exception subprocesses, duplicate checks, certificates and message monitoring, because they decide whether an integration survives its first month.
